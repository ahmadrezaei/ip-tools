The question: “Can the BGP map show the current path? It is so unclear.”

The answer: BGP Map now opens on Your route, the chain of networks your own traffic crosses. The worldwide graph is still there as All routes, now with a one-line summary and a legend.

Your route (iPhone)

The chain runs top to bottom, from you to the destination. On a 393 pt screen that leaves room for full names and hop details, works with Dynamic Type, and reads like a list. Solid blue rail = measured. Dashed rail = not measured. The destination is blue, as it is in All routes. Data: a real trace from this Mac to 8.8.8.8 (hops 1–3 private, 4–7 Cloudflare, 8–11 Google). iPad

At regular width the chain runs left to right, ending at the destination on the right, the same direction as the All routes graph. Selecting a network opens the inspector, which gains an “On your route” section. “Show 11 hops” is expanded here, with hops grouped under their network.

Ping: choose how many pings

Owner: “on default 10 ping, add option user can change how many ping must be”. A count chip now sits under the target bar on the Ping screen. It holds the same value as Ping Settings › Count, so the two stay in sync. Presets: 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, Until stopped, plus Custom… (1–10,000, remembered). The chip is disabled while a run is active, and the status line shows progress.

Notes

  • Where the chain comes from, in order: a trace handed over from the Traceroute tab (≤ 10 min old) → a RIS route that contains your AS → a fresh quick traceroute (2 probes, 1.5 s timeout, stops early once Google’s network is reached or after 5 silent hops).
  • Collapse rules: leading private hops belong to You. Consecutive hops in one AS merge. Silent or unknown hops between two hops of the same AS are absorbed. Between different ASes they are dropped and counted. A repeated AS folds back (no loops). When your public-IP AS differs from the first traced AS (a VPN or tunnel, as on this Mac), both are shown, without inventing hops.
  • Honesty: every chain says how it was obtained (“Measured by traceroute”, “Seen in BGP from your network”, “from BGP” tags). A gap is shown as “?” when nothing trustworthy can fill it.
  • VoiceOver reads the chain as one sentence: “Your route to 8.8.8.8, measured by traceroute: from your network, AS 24940 Hetzner, through AS 13335 Cloudflare, to AS 15169 Google, the destination. 11 hops.”
  • Privacy: RIPEstat already sees your public IP on every request. whats-my-ip only echoes it back. It is kept in memory only. PRIVACY.md and About gain one sentence each (DESIGN §9).
